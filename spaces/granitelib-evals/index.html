How the numbers are combined

  • Charts put every score on a 0–100 scale. Cards that report 0–1 metrics (guardian-core, requirement-check) are multiplied by 100. Source tables keep each card's own numbers.
  • Margin is the Granite adapter's score minus the best decision model on that benchmark, in points. Pick a decision model under Compare to measure against that model instead.
  • Click the Granite chip in the legend to change the adapter. It defaults to "Best per benchmark", the highest-scoring adapter on each benchmark, which is how the guardian and requirement-check cards fill their "Best trained adapter" column. Picking a single adapter, such as the 30B aLoRA, shows that adapter everywhere; the hallucination detection card publishes LoRA adapters only, so an aLoRA choice falls back to the LoRA of the same size there and is flagged.
  • On guardian-core, decision-model scores come from the card's comparison table and Granite scores from its per-adapter tables, which report OOD Safety and Function Calling to two decimals.
  • Factuality Detection's headline metric is F1 on each of its two test sets, ELI5 and Biographies.
  • The RAG cards compare openjev/openjev, which they do not mark as hosted, plus three hosted models. The core and guardian cards compare four hosted models, including clef-flash, and not openjev.
