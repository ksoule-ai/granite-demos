Speech to Decisions

Live speech, sized up as you talk

Talk into your microphone. Parakeet turns your speech into text, and about once a second two models judge your last 25 words: gpt-oss-120b says what you're doing, and Jev screens for harmful content and answers every yes/no question. After 10 seconds of silence the board goes blank until you speak again. Your audio is sent to this Space and on to OpenRouter.

On the board:

  • What the speaker is doing, in 1 to 5 words
  • A safety check for harm, profanity and violence
  • Yes/no questions you can edit before you talk
